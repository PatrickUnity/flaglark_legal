AprilDayStudio ("we", "us", or "our") provides Minesweeper Classic: Flaglark ("Flaglark" or the "Game"). This Policy explains information handling in the Android app com.flaglark.minesweeper and your choices. Contact us at AprilDayStudiogame@gmail.com.

No Game account is required. Game progress is stored on your device. The current version does not enable live advertising, in-app purchases, or paid subscriptions. A reward preview is not an advertisement, and the in-game feedback form does not send a support message to us.

1. Information handled

Local game data. The Game stores your current and saved boards, difficulty and board preferences, tutorial progress, game statistics and best times, daily-challenge completion, earned awards, daily streaks, installation and play dates, and settings such as sound, vibration, hints and display preferences in app-specific storage. This supports resuming play and retaining your choices. Our game logic does not upload these saves to a game server operated by us. There is no account-based cloud synchronization, chat, or online leaderboard.

Device information used for display and controls. The Game reads system language, display size and density, text scale, refresh rate, system-bar and cutout insets, keyboard visibility and related device capabilities to render the interface and respond to input. These readings are used locally by the Game's interface; our game logic does not send them to our own server.

In-game feedback. If you enter a rating or text in the feedback dialog, it remains in the current local application session. Confirming the dialog does not email it or upload it to us. It may be replaced by a later feedback entry and is not retained by the Game after its process ends. For a response from us, contact the support email instead. Choosing a separate Google Play review link opens Google's service, where information you submit is handled by Google and may be public under its review rules.

Support you choose to send. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to include. We use them to answer questions, investigate problems and handle privacy or other requests. Do not include unnecessary identity documents, payment information or other sensitive details. We request additional verification only when reasonably necessary.

Engine and platform services. The Game is built with Unity. Engine or platform services can handle technical information, such as device and operating-system information, app version, IP address and diagnostics, depending on the features enabled and the provider's own practices. This version does not enable Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, Unity cloud crash reporting or Firebase Analytics. Using the Unity engine is separate from using Unity's advertising service. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy for the services you use.

The Game does not ask for a name, phone number, password or payment-card details to play. It does not request access to contacts, SMS, microphone, camera or precise GPS location. Vibration is used for touch feedback. A network permission does not by itself mean that local saves are uploaded. We do not operate a remote push-notification service in this version.

2. Advertising, purchases and tracking

The current version has no enabled live advertising SDK, advertising-identifier collection for ads, targeted-advertising flow or in-game payment processing. Hint or revival screens may offer an explicitly labelled local reward preview. Selecting its completion control applies the displayed in-game result without requesting a live ad.

Premium pages currently show plan types and an unavailable-purchases notice. They do not start a payment, create a subscription or renew a plan. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising through the current Game.

If advertising, analytics or paid features are introduced, we will update the disclosures before the new processing begins and obtain any required consent. These documents and continued use do not authorize future charges or optional data processing in advance.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Information is handled to provide local gameplay and settings, adapt the interface, respond to communications, protect security and comply with applicable law. Sending a support request does not subscribe you to marketing email.

Where a legal basis is required, requested services and support may rely on contractual necessity or steps taken at your request; legal obligations and appropriately assessed legitimate interests may support necessary compliance and security processing. Processing that requires consent will rely on valid consent. Merely agreeing to user terms or continuing to use the Game is not a substitute for required consent.

4. Third parties and disclosure

Email providers process messages you send so that we can receive and respond to them. Google Play, your device or backup provider, Unity and websites you choose to visit handle information under their applicable roles and policies. This does not remove our responsibilities for the Game and the services we integrate.

We may disclose information we actually hold when necessary to comply with a valid legal request, protect lawful rights or complete a business transfer, subject to applicable notice, safeguards and consent requirements. Local saves that have never been provided to us are not available for us to disclose.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

Local saves remain on your device while the Game retains its app data. You can delete them through Android Settings → Apps → Flaglark → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data, or by normally uninstalling the Game. Labels vary by device. Progress and preferences may then be unrecoverable. Starting a new round does not delete all app data. Copies retained by your system or backup service must be managed through that service's settings.

Support correspondence is retained only as necessary to resolve the request, address disputes, prevent abuse and satisfy legal retention requirements. When those purposes and any lawful retention basis end, we delete or anonymize it. The period depends on the request, applicable limitation periods and security needs. You may request deletion without first uninstalling the Game.

We cannot remotely access or erase saves that remain solely on your device. There is no Game account to close. Data independently held by another provider is subject to that provider's retention and deletion processes; we will explain the relevant channels and assist where required.

6. Security and international processing

We use safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. Local saves use app-specific storage and do not have additional application-layer encryption. Protect access to your device and backups. No storage or transmission method is absolutely secure; we will meet applicable incident-notification duties.

Email, engine, platform or hosting providers may process information outside your country. Where required, we will use applicable transfer grounds and safeguards and provide required information or obtain required consent. Contact us for information about arrangements relevant to your request.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, copies, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent and opting out of legally defined sale, sharing or targeted advertising. Send requests to AprilDayStudiogame@gmail.com, identifying Flaglark and describing the request. We respond within applicable legal deadlines and may reasonably verify requests when necessary.

You may also use your device's app-data and backup controls and the privacy tools of services you use. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully based on consent before withdrawal. You may complain to a competent data-protection authority. We do not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Children and minors

The Game is not directed to children under 13 or anyone below the applicable minimum age in their location. Users who meet the minimum age but are still minors should use it with parental or legal-guardian guidance and any consent required by law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable minimum age. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so that we can investigate and take required protective or deletion steps.
